Due to large space needed for Parquet files storage, first download CSV files from Google Drive,
then create Parquet files with scripts/preprocess_flights.py,
and then create scoped, test, train and validation Parquet files using scripts/build_features.py

Alternatively, you can download already generated Parquet files from Google Drive
